Payroll Co., Ltd. and QueryPie Enter Technical Partnership in AI Security Field

~Collaborating on Security and Data Integration Technologies for the Safe and Rapid Deployment of AI Services~

(August 5, 2025, QueryPie Japan LLC)

Payroll Co., Ltd. (Headquarters: Koto-ku, Tokyo; President and Representative Director: Tetsuya Yuasa; hereinafter "Payroll") and QueryPie, Inc. (Headquarters: California, USA; CEO: Brant Hwang), a provider of an integrated data governance platform, along with its Japanese subsidiary QueryPie Japan LLC (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative: Keizo Arienobu; hereinafter "QueryPie"), are pleased to announce that they have agreed to a technical partnership in the fields of AI and security.

This partnership leverages QueryPie's security, access control, and data governance technologies, as well as its expertise in AI integration, in the development of AI-driven operational efficiency solutions being advanced by Payroll. This will enable the market introduction of safer and more reliable AI services, aiming to accelerate innovation in HR and payroll operations.

Under this partnership, QueryPie will provide the following support to Payroll:

  1. Provision of security solutions and related knowledge

  2. Sharing of expertise on the latest AI integration

  3. Provision of implementation support framework, including the dispatch of specialized personnel

Through this partnership, Payroll aims to achieve the following outcomes:

  1. Development of secure generative AI, MCP (Model Context Protocol), and AI agent services, and establishment of an operational foundation

  2. Operational efficiency and advancement through AI integration across the entire service

  3. Accumulation and utilization of the latest AI knowledge within the company

  4. Building a framework aimed at increasing current development productivity by 5x and reducing costs by 30%

Payroll is accelerating its AI service deployment through new initiatives, including the AI integration with the cloud human resource management system "Kaonavi" announced in June 2025. (*1) This partnership aims to enhance security and governance in such advanced initiatives.

[Company Overview]

■ About Payroll Co., Ltd.

Established on April 1, 1989. Since its founding, the company has primarily provided BPO (Business Process Outsourcing) for payroll processing operations targeting large enterprises, and is entrusted with payroll processing operations for 260 companies and 1.12 million employees (as of the end of March 2024). Payroll's general-purpose payroll processing service "HR BPaaS" is a service that integrates its independently developed cloud HR and payroll software with payroll processing BPO, accommodating the complex payroll processing logic specific to each customer while
promoting the standardization of operations common to all customers, which is characterized by its high flexibility and scalability.
In Japan, where the working population is declining, Payroll aims to become a soft infrastructure company that handles highly specialized operational tasks for HR departments, supporting an environment where HR can focus on more strategic work.

■ About QueryPie

Founded in 2016 in Silicon Valley, USA. It began as a data security solution and now offers an integrated security control hub that spans AI, cloud infrastructure, SaaS, and on-premises environments. QueryPie is equipped with security features that enable integrated access control and auditing of all critical enterprise IT systems—such as databases, systems, Kubernetes, and web applications—in one place, and complies with major international security standards including GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, and ISO 27001/27701.
Recently, it launched the AI security automation platform "QueryPie AI Hub" and is also working to establish new security standards for the AI era. It is creating a new digital standard that provides security and governance for all connections, and with backing from prominent investors including Salesforce Ventures, it is rapidly expanding its business primarily in the Japanese, US, and Southeast Asian markets.
