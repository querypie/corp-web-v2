5. Security and Governance: Controlling AI Intelligence

As intelligence increases, so do the risks posed by AI (inappropriate inference of confidential information, execution of unauthorized code, etc.). Anthropic states in theofficial system cardthat the Mythos-class model "has reached a threshold that poses significant risks," making it urgent for enterprises to establish governance.

The **MCP-PAM (Model Context Protocol - Privileged Access Management)** advocated by QueryPie AI serves as a "shield" when powerful AI like Claude Fable 5 accesses critical corporate assets.

  • Context-aware access control: A flow where the AI explains why it needs that data, and a human approves it.

  • Full audit logs: Records all of the AI's thought and execution processes, enabling post-verification.

  • 30-day data retention support: Anthropic has established a new 30-day data retention policy for traffic from Mythos-class models. Enterprises are required to have a data management infrastructure that supports this.
