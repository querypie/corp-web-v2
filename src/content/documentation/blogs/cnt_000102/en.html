QueryPie AI collaborates with TerraSky Corporation to
announce AI agent "mitoco Buddy"

~ Accelerating enterprise AI utilization with an MCP-compatible AI agent service ~

(2025年11月7日 QueryPie AI合同会社)

QueryPie AI LLC (Headquarters: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Co-Representatives: Brant Hwang; hereinafter "QueryPie AI") announces a collaboration with TerraSky Corporation (Headquarters: Chuo-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director, CEO and President: Hideya Sato; hereinafter "TerraSky") in the AI agent business.

Through this collaboration, QueryPie AI's enterprise AI platform "QueryPie AI Platform (AIP)" has been adopted for TerraSky's new service "mitoco Buddy." As an MCP (Model Context Protocol)-compatible AI agent service, "mitoco Buddy" integrates the proliferation of cloud services within enterprises, promotes information sharing and data utilization, and supports accelerated decision-making and business automation.

■ Background

Due to the shift to cloud-based enterprise system environments, numerous tools have proliferated, and utilizing their data in a cross-sectional manner has become an urgent task for promoting DX. However, challenges such as "environment fragmentation," "complexity of security management," and "cost efficiency" still remain in integrating cloud services built on different technologies.

To resolve these issues, QueryPie AI developed "QueryPie AIP," which enables cross-cloud data integration and AI model operation. Based on an MCP-compatible AI architecture, it provides a mechanism to utilize enterprise data safely and comprehensively. This collaboration with TerraSky, which has a wealth of experience in the Salesforce ecosystem, will realize an AI agent service optimized for Japanese enterprises.

■ About "mitoco Buddy"

"mitoco Buddy" is an enterprise AI agent service developed utilizing the advanced AI infrastructure technology of QueryPie AIP.

The main features are as follows.

  • Can integrate with approximately 50 services via MCP

    • Provides MCP-based integration with a wide range of cloud services, including Salesforce, Slack, Gmail, and Microsoft 365. Leverages existing work environments to achieve smooth integration and connectivity. Services not officially supported can also be integrated through custom configuration.

  • Add your own dedicated agents

    • You can add custom agents such as "Register opportunities in Salesforce," "Scan business cards," and "Generate reports." It also supports recurring and scheduled executions via a scheduler.

  • Use multiple LLMs for different purposes

    • By configuring multiple LLMs with different strengths depending on type and version, such as ChatGPT and Claude, you can switch between them as needed.

  • Enterprise-grade security

    • Ensures security by centrally managing user activity trends, MCP, and data usage to visualize AI operations. Provides one-stop support for enterprise AI governance and security audit compliance.

■ Objectives of the Collaboration and Future Developments

Through this collaboration, QueryPie AI provides the technical foundation of "QueryPie AIP," MCP integration, and architecture design in the security domain, supporting the reliability and scalability of "mitoco Buddy." Additionally, technical support through the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) service comprehensively supports enterprises from AI adoption to production operation.

QueryPie AI is driven by the belief that "the core of software is the people it supports," and aims to provide solutions that allow Japanese enterprises to experience the true value of AI without barriers of complexity or cost. This collaboration with TerraSky, which has extensive track record in the Salesforce ecosystem, will deliver AI agent services optimized for the Japanese market.

Through "mitoco Buddy," TerraSky promotes AI utilization across various areas of corporate activity, including sales support, operational efficiency, and customer support. A pre-release version is scheduled for December 1, 2025, with the general release planned for the end of December.

The two companies will proceed with deployment across a wide range of industries, including finance, manufacturing, and services, aiming for cumulative adoption by 300 companies over the three-year period through 2028.

We are confident that this service, combining MCP-based integration with enterprise-grade security, will be a significant step forward in accelerating AI adoption for Japanese enterprises.
