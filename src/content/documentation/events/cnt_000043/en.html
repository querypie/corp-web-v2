Companies in finance, commerce, platforms, and other industries hold various sensitive information across thousands or tens of thousands of databases. Naturally, the cloud, data warehouses, and various BI tools used by companies make the security architecture for managing these increasingly complex. QueryPie's DB Access Control (QueryPie DAC) plays a central role in strengthening security for accumulated sensitive information, simplifying access control and management, and ensuring security stability. We will explain how to safely manage vast amounts of sensitive information and data in a multi-cloud environment.
