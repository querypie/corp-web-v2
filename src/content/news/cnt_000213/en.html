QueryPie AI today announced a strategic partnership with Ashisuto AI Terasu Co., Ltd. to accelerate enterprise AI adoption and strengthen AI governance for organizations across Japan.

Through this partnership, Ashisuto AI Terasu will provide and sell QueryPie AIP, QueryPie AI's enterprise AI agent platform with built-in AI governance capabilities, either directly to customers or through its sales partners in the Japanese market. The company will also establish a framework to offer AI agent development services under its own brand based on customer requirements and deployment models.

Starting with the sales, implementation, and operational support of QueryPie AIP, the two companies will jointly help enterprises build secure and practical environments for utilizing AI agents and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). In addition, AI applications provided by QueryPie AI, including Lingo and NotePie, will also be offered through this partnership.

As generative AI and AI agents become increasingly integrated into enterprise workflows, more AI agents are connecting to internal and external systems through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). As a result, organizations require comprehensive AI governance capabilities to monitor AI usage, manage access permissions, maintain audit logs, protect sensitive information, and enforce organizational security policies.

Ashisuto AI Terasu was established in January 2026 to specialize decades of experience in IT solution consulting and implementation into the AI domain. The company provides end-to-end services spanning AI strategy, proof of concept (PoC), production deployment, operations, and continuous optimization, positioning itself as an Outcome Integrator that helps organizations transform AI initiatives into measurable business outcomes.

By combining Ashisuto AI Terasu's expertise in AI implementation, customer success, and partner ecosystem with QueryPie AI's AI governance technology, the two companies aim to help Japanese enterprises adopt AI securely and sustainably.

Partnership Highlights

The collaboration will focus on the following initiatives.

1. Delivery, Sales, and Implementation Support for QueryPie AIP

Ashisuto AI Terasu will provide and sell QueryPie AIP while supporting customers throughout the entire deployment lifecycle, from evaluation and implementation to post-deployment operations, enabling enterprises to utilize AI agents and MCP securely.

2. Flexible Delivery Models Based on Customer Requirements

Ashisuto AI Terasu will establish a framework to deliver QueryPie AIP as part of its own branded services according to customer requirements and deployment preferences, providing flexible implementation options tailored to each organization's environment.

3. Market Expansion Through Sales Partners

In addition to direct customer engagement, Ashisuto AI Terasu will deliver QueryPie AIP through its network of sales partners. Together, the two companies will strengthen their sales and implementation capabilities to expand access to AI governance platforms across a broader range of enterprises.

4. Joint Integration and Custom Development

The companies will collaborate on feature configuration, external system integration, and custom development based on each customer's operational requirements and IT environment. Their goal is to provide enterprise AI platforms tailored to practical business needs.

About QueryPie AIP

QueryPie AIP is an enterprise AI agent platform with built-in AI governance capabilities. It enables organizations to centrally manage and monitor AI agents and MCP-based access while empowering business teams to utilize AI securely and giving IT and security teams the governance controls they require.

Key capabilities include:

  • Centrally managing AI agent and MCP tool access permissions at the organizational level

  • Providing visibility into who can access which data through which AI agents and tools

  • Recording AI agent activities and MCP access events as comprehensive audit logs

  • Enforcing organizational policies for AI conversations and tool integrations

  • Detecting and automatically removing sensitive information such as API keys and credit card numbers from AI interactions

  • Supporting MCP connectivity with leading development environments and AI agents, including Cursor and Claude Desktop
